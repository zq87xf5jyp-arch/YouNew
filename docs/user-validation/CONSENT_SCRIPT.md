# Participant consent script — draft

YouNew is testing whether newcomers can find and understand official
information. Participation is voluntary. The session records only task outcome,
source-open, counts of wrong turns/external searches, whether help was needed,
duration and safety-stop status under a random session code.

Do not enter or say BSN, medical information, passwords, DigiD credentials,
contact details or private form text during the study.

The research record is retained for no more than 90 days. It is not used for
advertising, personalization or AI training. You may stop at any time. The
approved withdrawal procedure and human contact must be inserted here before
recruitment.

Consent result:

- [ ] I received the approved information and had an opportunity to ask questions.
- [ ] I voluntarily consent to this specific study.
- [ ] I understand that I may stop at any time.

The script is not recruitment-ready until the missing human contact and privacy
decisions are completed.
