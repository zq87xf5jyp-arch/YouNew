# First 30 days after an approved launch

## Days 0–1

- Check home, `/nl/`, search, one category, one organization, all ten city-guide locales and both business registration routes from desktop and mobile.
- Submit one synthetic staging/production-safe business test only under the approved test procedure; verify stored receipt and Admin visibility, then handle the record under the retention policy.
- Review 4xx/5xx, form failures and consented analytics ingest. Roll back on a critical privacy, data-loss or application failure.
- Submit the sitemap and inspect the priority URLs in Search Console.

## Days 2–7

- Review Search Console coverage daily; classify evidence rather than chasing every exclusion.
- Review first-party page views, organization-profile page views, official-source clicks, form starts and stored receipts.
- Review the first 50 Phase 3 queue records manually; update only with authoritative field-level evidence.
- Publish at most the evidence-ready items from the bilingual content plan.
- Keep all 30 prospects on hold until consent/purpose evidence exists. No automatic outreach.

## Days 8–14

- Compare top impressions with existing verified pages and strengthen internal links where intent matches.
- Interview willing inbound businesses; record requested outcomes, not feature promises.
- Measure application completion and review time. Fix the highest-evidence friction point.
- Recheck mobile performance and layout for pages receiving real traffic.

## Days 15–21

- Promote verified wave-2 records only after source, date, reviewer and fields are recorded.
- Refresh city guides when an official source changes; retain the prior evidence in the review history.
- Report paid-interest opt-ins separately from free registrations and separately from revenue.

## Days 22–30

- Produce a 30-day evidence report: indexed pages, organic clicks, consented product use, stored business applications, approved profiles, interest opt-ins, incidents and rollback time.
- Decide whether to run a small permission-based outreach test. The decision requires an eligible channel list and separate sending approval.
- Keep, revise or stop each content/business experiment based on measured outcomes.

## Daily/weekly thresholds

- Critical: any PII leak, unauthorized analytics, persistent form-save failure or incorrect official claim → stop/rollback.
- Daily: availability, form receipts, 5xx, sitemap/robots, consent ingest.
- Twice weekly: indexing, search queries, source-change queue, application review backlog.
- Weekly: verified-content ratio, non-brand organic clicks, profile-to-business outbound rate, registration conversion, median review time and Business/Premium opt-in rate.
