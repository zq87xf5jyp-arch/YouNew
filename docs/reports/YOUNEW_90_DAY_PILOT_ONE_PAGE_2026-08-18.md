# YouNew 90-Day Employee Onboarding Pilot

Owner-review one-page overview — 2026-08-18  
Status: local implementation; links below are target production routes and must not be presented as live until deployment is approved and completed

## The offer

Give international employees one clear place to start in the Netherlands. YouNew turns verified public information about registration, healthcare, housing, work, and daily life into practical, location-aware steps.

**Founding pilot — €1,500 excl. VAT. Fixed scope confirmed before contract.**

Available for the first three accepted pilots. No fake discount, countdown, or claim about existing clients is used.

## Included for 90 days

- One branded onboarding hub.
- Ten verified practical routes.
- One selected Dutch city or region.
- Official sources and visible verification dates.
- Consent-based usage analytics.
- One summary report and one final report.
- One client revision round.
- A 90-day content-maintenance period.

## Explicitly excluded

- HRIS, SSO, or custom enterprise integrations.
- Individual legal, medical, immigration, or tax advice.
- Guaranteed employee outcomes.
- Custom translations outside the approved scope.
- Live checkout: proposal, contract, and manual invoice are used.

## Pilot review sequence

1. Confirm organization, employee cohort, selected region, and route scope.
2. Approve proposal and contract; invoice manually.
3. Launch the branded hub only after content and technical review.
4. Maintain source dates and review consent-based aggregate usage.
5. Deliver summary and final reports; decide whether to stop, revise, or extend.

## Safety position

YouNew provides practical navigation, not individual legal, medical, or tax advice. Critical guidance links to official sources and displays its latest verification date. Paid placement never changes factual guidance, emergency information, or editorial conclusions, and sponsored content is labelled.
