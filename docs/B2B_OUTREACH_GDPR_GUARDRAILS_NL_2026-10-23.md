# B2B outreach guardrails — Netherlands

Date: 2026-10-23. Operational guidance, not legal advice.

## Default rule

Do not send an unsolicited commercial email, message or contact-form submission merely because a company website or contact page is public. The public route can support company verification; it does not by itself establish consent or a lawful outreach channel.

A prospect moves from `verified` to `ready_for_contact` only when the tracker contains one of these evidence types:

1. recorded prior consent for YouNew's specific commercial approach;
2. an address or channel that the company consciously published for receiving this type of proposal, with a captured source and review date;
3. an existing-customer situation reviewed against the narrow similar-products exception.

No row in the current 30-company wave has that evidence. Therefore all rows remain `hold_do_not_contact`, even where an official contact route was observed.

## Minimum message controls after approval

- Identify YouNew and the commercial purpose immediately.
- Make the free, source-checked profile the main offer.
- Do not imply endorsement, guaranteed ranking, traffic, leads or revenue.
- Provide a free and simple objection/unsubscribe route in every message.
- Record when, where and why the channel was considered eligible.
- Stop immediately after an objection and retain only the minimal suppression record.
- Do not add personal email addresses, names or phone numbers from third-party enrichment without a documented legal basis and retention rule.

## Evidence model

`found → verified → ready_for_contact → sent → replied → interested → registered`

- `found`: a company was present in the supplied governed list.
- `verified`: the supplied official page opened and company identity was observed.
- `ready_for_contact`: separate consent/purpose evidence was recorded.
- `sent`: a dated delivery record exists. A draft is not sent.
- `replied`: a dated inbound response exists.
- `interested`: the response contains explicit interest.
- `registered`: a stored YouNew application receipt is linked.
