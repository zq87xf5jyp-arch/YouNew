# High-intent guide programme — v19

Status: editorial plan. Candidate topics are not approved for publication and must not appear as completed guides until they pass the existing publication gate.

## Goal

Publish 30–50 practical, source-backed guides that answer urgent settlement questions. The first target is 40 guides: the 20 existing governed guide work items plus 20 research candidates below.

## Publication gate

Every guide must have:

1. a named responsible official source and source-check date;
2. audience and eligibility boundaries;
3. prerequisites, ordered steps, costs, timing and failure/escalation routes;
4. city or municipality variations where relevant;
5. plain-language summary and FAQ;
6. editorial review and human approval;
7. structured-data and accessibility validation;
8. a configured review interval and owner.

No AI draft, official-feed item or partner submission may bypass this gate.

## Feed policy for updates, events and vacancies

- Ingest only official or contractually authorised feeds.
- Store source, canonical URL, fetched time, publication time and licence/usage basis.
- New items enter `review`, never `published`.
- Deduplicate by canonical URL and source identifier.
- Expire events and vacancies automatically, but require editorial approval for public visibility.
- Keep sponsored distribution separate from editorial ranking and label it visibly.

## Multilingual and local SEO gate

- English is the canonical reviewed source until each translation is human-reviewed.
- Do not index machine-only translations.
- Local landing pages require unique local facts, responsible sources and a current review date; template-only city pages remain noindex.
- Add `hreflang` only when equivalent reviewed pages exist.
