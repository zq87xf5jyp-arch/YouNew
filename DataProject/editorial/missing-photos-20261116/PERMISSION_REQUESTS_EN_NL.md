# Photo permission requests — drafts only

Prepared 2026-11-16. Not sent. Sending requires separate owner approval. A reply alone is insufficient unless the sender controls the photo rights and specifies permitted use; keep the actual permission evidence with the chosen file.

Four gaps still need a photo. The Supernova draft is retained only as an optional request for a newer, undecorated replacement; its CC BY 2.0 candidate does not depend on sending this message.

These drafts request a free license; do not accept paid terms or purchase stock. No iOS distribution is requested.

## Kaafi

Official contact source: https://kaafi.nl/contact-us

To: info@kaafi.nl

**EN subject:** Photo permission for the Kaafi listing on YouNew.nl

Hello Kaafi team,

We are preparing an independent English/Dutch listing of Kaafi on YouNew.nl. Could you provide one exterior or interior photograph that you own, or are authorised to license, and allow us to use it free of charge on this public website, including its listing cards and link previews? YouNew may have commercial features; this is not a request for endorsement or a paid placement.

Please identify the exact file, photographer and required credit; confirm that you control the necessary rights and permit online display, resizing, WebP conversion and responsive display crops. Please specify any limits, expiry, third-party rights or additional conditions. A non-expiring permission, or an existing CC BY / CC BY-SA license, would be helpful. We will review the terms before using the image and link to its source and license.

Thank you,
YouNew
