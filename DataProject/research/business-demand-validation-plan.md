# Business/Premium demand validation

Status: local implementation prepared; no paid plan is for sale.

## What is measurable now

The Dutch Add/Claim form contains an optional, explicit research section. A checked submission is stored through the existing protected inquiry pipeline with `offer_interest = other`; the selected research topics are encoded in the bounded campaign goal. The copy states that the features are unavailable, non-binding and create no price agreement.

Research topics:

- profile views and outbound-click insight;
- richer reviewed profile information and media;
- clearly labelled sponsored placement.

The free, source-checked profile remains the primary offer.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Form view — availability, not intent.
2. Form start — friction signal, not a lead.
3. Stored receipt — valid business application.
4. `offer_interest = other` — explicit non-binding Business/Premium interest.
5. Qualified response — a reviewed business conversation with stated need.
6. Paid evidence — signed order and settled invoice; nothing below this level is revenue.

## Questions for the first ten qualified businesses

- Which one recurring task would profile insights help you complete?
- Which reviewed profile fields are missing today?
- Would a clearly labelled sponsored position be useful if organic order stayed independent?
- What proof would you need before considering payment?
- Which monthly price range would be worth testing after the function exists?

Do not promise implementation dates or reporting capabilities during research.

## Illustrative revenue arithmetic — not a forecast

Formula: `registered businesses × paid-interest rate × assumed monthly price = illustrative MRR`.

| Registered businesses | Paid-interest assumption | Illustrative price | Arithmetic only |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 30 | 10% | €25/month | €75 MRR |
| 30 | 20% | €25/month | €150 MRR |

These examples do not represent demand, signed customers or expected income. Replace assumptions only after measured opt-ins, interviews and payment evidence exist.

## Buyer-relevant KPIs

- verified-current published records / total published records;
- non-brand organic clicks and indexed canonical pages;
- organization profile views and official-source/business outbound clicks;
- stored business applications and receipt success rate;
- application-to-approved-profile conversion and median review time;
- explicit paid-interest opt-ins / stored business applications;
- active registered businesses at 30/90 days;
- paid conversion, retained MRR and churn only after payment exists;
- privacy/security incidents, spam rate and rollback time.
