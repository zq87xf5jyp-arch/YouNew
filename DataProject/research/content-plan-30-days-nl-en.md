## Publishing cadence and measurement

- Publish no more than three substantial pieces per week; use the remaining days for source review, translation, internal links and updates.
- Every NL article links to its EN counterpart, relevant city guide, one category and only verified-current organizations.
- Measure Search Console impressions/clicks, consented page views, organization profile views and official-source clicks. Do not compare raw counts as if consent coverage were 100%.
- Pause a scheduled item when its evidence gate is not met; do not substitute generated facts.
